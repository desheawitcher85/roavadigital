## METHODOLOGY SECTION

## How Roava works.

Every Roava client gets the same four-part system. The work is the same whether you do $400K or $4M a year. The difference is volume.

### 1. A website built for one job: making the phone ring.

Fast, mobile-first, loads in under 2 seconds. Click-to-call buttons on every page. Service area maps that match the towns you actually drive to. Real photos of finished jobs, not stock photography. Trust signals visible above the fold: license number, BBB rating, insurance verification, real Google reviews.

No carousel sliders. No "Our Story" essay nobody reads. No hero video that adds 4 seconds to your load time. Every element exists to get a homeowner to call you.

### 2. Local SEO that gets you in the top 3.

We optimize your Google Business Profile, audit and clean up your local citations across the 30 directories that actually move the needle, and fix the NAP inconsistencies that are quietly tanking your rank. Then we publish content that ranks for what your customers actually search: "metal roof Roanoke," "hail damage repair Botetourt County," "gutter replacement Salem VA."

Most contractor websites target three keywords. We target 300, mapped by service and service area.

### 3. Reviews on autopilot.

We set up an automated system that asks happy customers for a review the same day the job ends. Most contractors leave 30% of their potential reviews on the table because they forget to ask in the moment. Roava clients average 8 to 12 new Google reviews per month within 90 days of launch.

More reviews means a higher local pack rank, higher click-through rate from search, and a higher conversion rate when a homeowner lands on your Google profile.

### 4. Call tracking so you actually know what works.

Every lead source gets its own tracked phone number. Google Business Profile, organic website, Google Ads, Local Service Ads. We see exactly where calls come from, how long they last, and whether they converted. You stop guessing and start optimizing.

> **Insider Tip:** Most agencies do not show you raw call data because their numbers would not hold up. Roava gives you full access from day one, including call recordings if you enable them.
